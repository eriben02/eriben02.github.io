Why a "$12/seat" CRM costs $36 a month for a solo founder.

monday CRM bills in fixed buckets starting at three seats, while Pipedrive and Close bill per actual user. Use the calculator below to see where the math flips.

The short version: For 1 user, Pipedrive ($14/mo Basic or $34/mo Growth) is up to 60% cheaper than monday CRM because monday CRM enforces a 3-seat minimum ($36/mo Basic [$12/seat annual, $18/seat monthly] or $51/mo Standard [$17/seat annual, $25/seat monthly] minimum). For 2 users, monday CRM still bills for 3 seats ($25.50/user/mo effective rate on Standard). At exactly 3, 5, or 10 users, however, monday CRM becomes the lowest-cost full-featured CRM at $51/mo (3 users) vs. $102/mo on Pipedrive Growth and $270/mo (+ $1,500 onboarding) on HubSpot Sales Pro.

Compare seat-bucket pricing for 1 to 15 users

monday CRM sells seats in fixed buckets of 3, 5, 10, and 15 seats. Adding a 4th user jumps your bill from 3 seats to 5 seats (+67%); adding a 6th user jumps your bill from 5 seats to 10 seats (+100%).

How to read this comparison: Lowest cost depends on whether your team size lands on a 1:1 seat count (1, 2, 4, 6–9 users) or cleanly matches monday CRM’s 3, 5, 10, or 15-seat buckets.

When to pick Pipedrive vs. monday CRM

Choose Pipedrive or Close CRM when you have 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, or 11–12 users—sizes where monday CRM forces you to pay for unused seats. Choose monday CRM when your headcount lands cleanly on 3, 5, 10, or 15 users.

Why monday CRM’s 3-seat minimum and 5-seat increments matter

monday.com’s pricing selector starts at 3 seats and steps in fixed increments (3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25...). Teams with 1, 2, 4, or 6–9 users pay for unallocated seats in their bucket.

Common questions

Can you buy 1 seat of monday CRM in 2026?

No. monday CRM enforces a mandatory 3-seat minimum on all paid plans. If you are a solo user on the $12/seat Basic plan ($18/seat billed monthly) or $17/seat Standard plan ($25/seat billed monthly), you must pay for 3 seats ($36/month annual or $54/month monthly on Basic; $51/month annual or $75/month monthly on Standard).

How do monday CRM seat buckets work when adding a 4th or 6th user?

monday CRM bills in fixed seat buckets of 3, 5, 10, and 15 seats. If you have 4 active users, you must pay for the 5-seat bucket; if you have 6 active users, you must pay for the 10-seat bucket.

Which CRM is best for a 1-person or 2-person sales team?

Pipedrive ($14–$34/user/month) and Close CRM Essentials ($35/user/month billed annually, $49/user/month billed monthly; or Solo at $9/month for 1 user) bill strictly 1:1 per active user with $0 mandatory onboarding fees, making them significantly more cost-effective than monday CRM or HubSpot Professional for 1–2 users.
