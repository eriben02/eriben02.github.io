Assumptions↓
  • USD, excluding VAT. Year 1 = 12 months of annual billing. Pipedrive monthly prices are not verified, so this page does not total them.
  • LeadBooster's annual price is per company. Smart Docs, Projects, Campaigns and Web Visitors publish a from-price only. Whether that price is per seat or per company is unknown, so a selected add-on is a floor (at least the from-price times 12), never $0.
  • LeadBooster, Smart Docs and Projects are included in Premium and Ultimate. That inclusion is a verified extra of $0, labeled Included.
  • Pipedrive has no built-in dialer. A Marketplace dialer is not added on these pages, and it is not entered as $0.
  • HubSpot Sales Hub Professional is $90/seat/month annual plus $1,500 onboarding. Enterprise is $150/seat/month annual plus $3,500 onboarding. Starter is $15/seat/month annual and $0 onboarding, from the onboarding FAQ already published on this site. Calling credits are not in the HubSpot total.

Common questions

Is Pipedrive or HubSpot Sales Hub cheaper for 5 seats?

On the default annual example, Pipedrive Growth is $2,340 and Sales Hub Professional is $6,900, which is $5,400 in seats plus $1,500 onboarding. Lowest Year-1 cost: Pipedrive. Change the plan or add an add-on with an unpublished price basis and the page stops naming a lowest option when that unknown could change the order.

What HubSpot prices are in this comparison?

Sales Hub Starter is $15 per seat per month annual with $0 onboarding. Professional is $90 per seat per month annual plus $1,500 onboarding. Enterprise is $150 per seat per month annual plus $3,500 onboarding. Calling credits are not included.

Why are some Pipedrive add-ons shown as at least?

Campaigns, Web Visitors, Projects and Smart Docs publish a from-price, not whether it is per seat or per company. The Year-1 line uses at least that from-price times 12. It is not counted as $0, and no lowest option is named while that choice is open. Pipedrive monthly prices are unverified and are not used.
