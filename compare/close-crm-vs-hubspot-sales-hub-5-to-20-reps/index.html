Common questions

Does Close CRM charge an onboarding or implementation fee?

No. Close CRM charges $0 mandatory onboarding across Essentials ($35/seat/mo annual), Growth ($99/seat/mo annual), and Scale ($139/seat/mo annual), and includes built-in VoIP calling and SMS capabilities.

What is HubSpot Sales Hub Professional's mandatory onboarding fee in 2026?

HubSpot Sales Hub Professional requires a mandatory one-time $1,500 onboarding fee ($3,500 on Sales Hub Enterprise) in addition to $90/seat/month billed annually.
