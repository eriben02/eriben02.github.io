What we are using for the fee facts

  • Stripe: a cardholder dispute is modeled at a $15 fee. Stripe has also described a separate $15 counter fee when a dispute is challenged without Chargeback Protection. That counter fee is covered inside the $30 evidence-prep line below, so it is not added a second time on top of the $15 dispute fee.
  • Shopify Payments: modeled at $15 per dispute. Shopify refunds that fee in the US when you win. In several other regions the fee is not returned. This calculator keeps the fee in the loss so the total is not optimistic.
  • Paddle: public notes put the dispute fee around $15 to $20. The Paddle option uses $20.
  • High-volume or high-risk: modeled at $25 per dispute, before any monitoring program penalty.
  • Manual in-house: a 20% win rate, plus $30 per dispute for evidence prep and counter handling. At the default volume that prep is 156 x $30 = $4,680, which is more than the $3,744 gross recovered.
  • Pay-on-performance recovery (Chargeflow in this model): 25% of recovered dispute dollars ($0/mo base). Does not refund the processor's $15 to $20 dispute fee when a dispute is lost, and a 65% win rate is a planning benchmark. Actual recovery varies by dispute reason code.
  • Percent of all sales: 0.40% of yearly GMV. The calculator treats that fee as the lower cost only when it replaces disputed order value and dispute fees. Stripe Radar for Fraud Teams is often billed per screened payment. We do not treat 0.40% as Radar's only price.
  • Shopify Protect: covers eligible Shop Pay orders in the US. It does not cover a standard card gateway or PayPal dispute, so it is not the dollar winner for the whole volume.
  • Pre-dispute alert networks (Ethoca, Verifi RDR, Chargeblast): priced per alert and quoted. This model does not invent an alert fee, so they are listed beside the calculated options and are not picked as the dollar winner.

Sources: Stripe disputes, Stripe Radar, Shopify chargebacks, Chargeflow.

Common questions

How much is a Stripe or Shopify dispute fee?

This calculator uses $15 per dispute for Stripe and for Shopify Payments. Shopify refunds that fee in the US when you win. In several other regions it does not. Paddle is modeled at $20, inside a $15 to $20 range. A high-risk processor is modeled at $25. Stripe's separate $15 counter fee, when you contest without Chargeback Protection, is covered inside the $30 evidence-prep line and is not added a second time.

What does Chargeflow charge?

In this model a pay-on-performance tool such as Chargeflow has a $0 monthly fee and a $0 setup fee, then 25% of the dispute dollars it recovers. It does not refund the processor's $15 to $20 dispute fee when a dispute is lost. The 65% figure is a planning benchmark. Actual recovery varies by dispute reason code.

Why compare a 0.40% fee on all sales?

0.40% of yearly sales is $11,520 on $2.88 million, charged whether or not an order is disputed. A 25% fee only on the dollars actually recovered is $3,042 in the default scenario. Stripe Radar for Fraud Teams is often priced per screened payment, so do not read 0.40% as its only rate.
