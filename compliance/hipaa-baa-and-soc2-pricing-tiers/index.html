Which software plans actually sign a HIPAA BAA (and what SOC 2 really costs).

Most vendors lock HIPAA Business Associate Agreements and SAML SSO behind $20,000+ Enterprise contracts. A handful include them on self-serve plans, and Vanta or Drata quotes rarely include your CPA auditor bill.

The short version: Self-serve HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) are available on Jotform Gold ($99/mo billed annually, $129/mo monthly, 1-user account) and 1Password Business ($8.99/user/mo billed annually [$107.88/yr], which also includes SAML SSO with no Enterprise markup), whereas HubSpot ($150/seat + $3,500 onboarding), Zendesk ($115/agent Suite Pro), and Intercom ($132/seat Expert) require upper-tier plans. For SOC 2 Type II compliance automation, Vanta and Drata do not publish public self-serve prices; custom list quotes typically start around $9,000–$15,000+/yr (frequently negotiated down by 30%–50%), while Comp AI ($2,400–$12,000/yr) and Openlane (~$5,400/yr) cost less, plus a mandatory $6,500–$15,000+ external CPA auditor fee billed separately.

What IT buyers need to know about compliance quotes

Why SOC 2 automation requires two separate budgets

Compliance automation software (Vanta, Drata, Comp AI) continuously monitors your cloud controls and collects evidence, but AICPA rules require an independent licensed CPA firm to perform the audit and issue the official SOC 2 Type I or Type II report ($6,500–$15,000+ billed separately).

Common questions

Which Jotform plan includes a signed HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA)?

Jotform includes HIPAA compliance and an automated Business Associate Agreement (BAA) starting on its self-serve Gold plan ($99/month billed annually, $129/month billed monthly, 1-user account) and Enterprise plan (multi-user), avoiding the $20,000+ custom Enterprise contract required by many form builders.

Does Vanta or Drata pricing include the SOC 2 CPA auditor fee?

No. Vanta and Drata are compliance automation platforms that collect evidence and monitor controls, but an independent AICPA-licensed CPA firm must still perform the audit and issue the SOC 2 Type I or Type II report, which typically costs an additional $6,500 to $15,000+ per year.

How much discount can you negotiate on Vanta or Drata in 2026?

Initial custom quotes from Vanta and Drata ($9,000–$15,000+/year for startups) are frequently negotiated down by 30% to 50%, especially when comparing against lower-cost alternatives like Comp AI ($2.4k–$12k) or Openlane (~$5.4k).
