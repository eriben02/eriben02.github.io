Frequently Asked Questions (Last Verified October 25, 2026)

Quick Reference (Updated 2026-10-25): Every answer below starts with the exact 2026 dollar figure, percentage rate, or contract notice requirement verified against official vendor schedules and user contract audits.

Can you buy 1 seat of monday CRM in 2026?

No. monday CRM enforces a mandatory 3-seat minimum on all paid plans. If you are a solo user on the $12/seat Basic plan or $17/seat Standard plan (billed annually), you must pay for 3 seats ($36/month or $51/month).

How do monday CRM seat buckets work when adding a 4th or 6th user?

monday CRM bills in fixed seat buckets of 3, 5, 10, and 15 seats. If you have 4 active users, you must pay for the 5-seat bucket; if you have 6 active users, you must pay for the 10-seat bucket.

Which CRM is best for a 1-person or 2-person sales team?

Pipedrive ($14–$34/user/month) and Close CRM ($49/user/month) bill strictly 1:1 per active user with $0 mandatory onboarding fees, making them significantly more cost-effective than monday CRM or HubSpot Professional for 1–2 users.
