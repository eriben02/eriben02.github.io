Frequently Asked Questions (Last Verified October 25, 2026)

Quick Reference (Updated 2026-10-25): Every answer below starts with the exact 2026 dollar figure, percentage rate, or contract notice requirement verified against official vendor schedules and user contract audits.

Which B2B SaaS tools charge mandatory onboarding fees in 2026?

HubSpot charges mandatory one-time onboarding fees on its Professional and Enterprise tiers: $1,500 for Sales Hub Professional, $3,500 for Sales Hub Enterprise, $3,000 for Marketing Hub Professional, and $7,000 for Marketing Hub Enterprise (verified Oct 2026). Alternatives like Close CRM, Pipedrive, and Freshdesk charge $0 for onboarding.

How does Intercom's $0.99 per Fin AI resolution pricing work?

Intercom charges a per-seat monthly fee ($29 Essential, $85 Advanced, or $132 Expert billed annually) plus $0.99 for every customer conversation resolved by its Fin AI agent (and $9.99 for sales qualification). If Fin AI resolves 2,000 tickets a month, it adds $1,980/month ($23,760/year) to your seat invoice.

Why does monday CRM cost more than advertised for 1 or 2 users?

monday CRM enforces a 3-seat minimum and bills in fixed seat buckets (3, 5, 10, 15 seats). A solo user on the $17/seat/month Standard plan must pay for 3 seats ($51/month or $612/year). For 1–2 users, Pipedrive ($14–$34/seat) or Close CRM ($49/seat) is cheaper because they bill per exact seat.

What happens to subscriber card tokens if Paddle or Stripe terminates my account?

With Stripe (2.9% + $0.30), you are the merchant and own the customer relationship, allowing PGP-encrypted card token exports unless terminated for fraud. With a Merchant of Record like Paddle (5% + $0.50), Paddle is the legal seller of record and owns the card vault; if Paddle terminates your account on a risk review, you cannot export card tokens and every subscriber must re-enter their payment details.
