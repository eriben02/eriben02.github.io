What we found reading the fine print

Before signing an annual SaaS order form, check four clauses that rarely appear on the public pricing grid: mandatory onboarding fees, seat-bucket minimums, uncapped AI resolution meters, and 30-to-60 day non-renewal notice deadlines.

  • HubSpot’s onboarding fee adds 28% to a 5-seat Sales Pro deal: HubSpot Sales Hub Professional ($90/seat/month billed annually) requires a mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee ($3,500 on Enterprise), bringing five seats to $6,900 in Year 1 compared with $2,940/year on Pipedrive Professional ($0 onboarding).
  • Intercom and Zendesk AI meters can exceed your seat bill: At 3,000 monthly support conversations with 50% AI resolution (1,500 resolutions/month), Intercom Fin ($0.99/resolution) adds $1,485/month ($17,820/year) on top of agent seats. Zendesk Suite includes only 5–10 automated resolutions per agent/month before charging $1.50 committed or $2.00 pay-as-you-go per verified resolution.
  • monday CRM’s 3-seat minimum triples the price for solo founders: A single user on monday CRM Basic ($12/seat/month billed annually, $18/seat/month billed monthly) or Standard ($17/seat/month annual, $25/seat/month monthly) must pay for three seats ($36–$51/month annual or $54–$75/month monthly), whereas Pipedrive Lite/Essential bills one seat at $14/month ($168/year). The math flips in monday CRM’s favor at exactly 3, 5, or 10 users.
  • Merchant of Record platforms own your card vault: Stripe and Shopify Payments can hold payouts for 120–180 days or apply a 25% rolling reserve on risk flags, but you own the customer relationship. With a Merchant of Record like Paddle (5% + $0.50), Paddle is the seller of record—if Paddle terminates your account on a risk review, you cannot export subscriber card tokens.
  • Self-serve HIPAA BAAs exist if you know where to look: Jotform signs a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement on its self-serve Gold plan ($99/month billed annually, $129/month monthly, 1-user account), and 1Password Business includes SAML SSO at $8.99/user/month billed annually ($107.88/year; $10.99/user/month monthly) with no Enterprise SSO markup. Meanwhile, Vanta and Drata list quotes ($9k–$15k+/year) are routinely negotiated down by 30%–50% and never include the separate $6,500–$15,000+ CPA auditor fee.

Common questions

Which B2B SaaS tools charge mandatory onboarding fees in 2026?

HubSpot charges mandatory one-time onboarding fees on its Professional and Enterprise tiers: $1,500 for Sales Hub Professional, $3,500 for Sales Hub Enterprise, $3,000 for Marketing Hub Professional, and $7,000 for Marketing Hub Enterprise (verified Oct 2026). Alternatives like Close CRM, Pipedrive, and Freshdesk charge $0 for onboarding.

How does Intercom's $0.99 per Fin AI resolution pricing work?

Intercom charges a per-seat monthly fee ($29 Essential, $85 Advanced, or $132 Expert billed annually) plus $0.99 for every customer conversation resolved by its Fin AI agent (and $9.99 for sales qualification). If Fin AI resolves 2,000 tickets a month, it adds $1,980/month ($23,760/year) to your seat invoice.

Why does monday CRM cost more than advertised for 1 or 2 users?

monday CRM enforces a 3-seat minimum and bills in fixed seat buckets (3, 5, 10, 15 seats). A solo user on the $12/seat Basic ($18/seat monthly) or $17/seat Standard ($25/seat monthly) plan must pay for 3 seats ($36/month annual or $54/month monthly on Basic). For 1–2 users, Pipedrive ($14–$34/seat) or Close CRM Essentials ($35/user/month annual, $49/user/month monthly) is cheaper because they bill per exact seat.

What happens to subscriber card tokens if Paddle or Stripe terminates my account?

With Stripe (2.9% + $0.30), you are the merchant and own the customer relationship, allowing PGP-encrypted card token exports unless terminated for fraud. With a Merchant of Record like Paddle (5% + $0.50), Paddle is the legal seller of record and owns the card vault; if Paddle terminates your account on a risk review, you cannot export card tokens and every subscriber must re-enter their payment details.
