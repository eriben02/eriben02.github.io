3 Verified HubSpot Contract Traps to Check Before Signing

1. "Monthly Payment" Is Still a Binding 12-Month Contract With Auto-Renewal

All HubSpot Professional and Enterprise agreements are annual commitments that auto-renew by default, even when paid in monthly installments. On day 1 of the auto-renewal window, you are legally liable for the full 12-month renewal balance.

Frequently Asked Questions (Verified Oct 2026)

Quick Reference: Every answer below starts with the exact 2026 dollar figure, percentage rate, or contract notice requirement verified against official vendor schedules and user contract audits.

How much is the mandatory HubSpot onboarding fee in 2026?

In 2026, HubSpot charges a mandatory one-time onboarding fee of $1,500 for Sales Hub Professional, $3,500 for Sales Hub Enterprise, $3,000 for Marketing Hub Professional, and $7,000 for Marketing Hub Enterprise when purchasing directly.

Can you cancel a HubSpot monthly payment plan at any time?

On HubSpot Starter, yes. However, on HubSpot Professional and Enterprise tiers, selecting 'monthly billing' ($100/seat/mo instead of $90/seat/mo on Sales Pro) is still a binding 12-month annual contract paid in monthly installments, with automatic annual renewal unless cancelled prior to the notice deadline.

How do you waive the HubSpot $1,500 or $3,000 onboarding fee?

You can waive HubSpot's direct onboarding fee by purchasing your license through an accredited HubSpot Solutions Partner (agency/consultant) and signing an onboarding scope with them, or by switching to a zero-onboarding CRM such as Close CRM or Pipedrive.

How much does HubSpot Sales Hub Professional cost for 5 users in Year 1?

For 5 core sales seats billed annually at $90/seat/month ($450/month or $5,400/year) plus the mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee, HubSpot Sales Hub Professional costs $6,900 in Year 1 (excluding taxes and calling credit overages).
