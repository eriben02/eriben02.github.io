Why B2B Wix Store Owners Use the Jotform Wix App Instead of Basic Contact Forms

Standard website contact forms work fine for simple messages, but wholesale and B2B merchants on Wix routinely need multi-line SKU quote requests, volume tier logic, tax-exempt certificate uploads, signed purchase agreements, or HIPAA-compliant intake. Embedding Jotform through the native Jotform Wix App solves four practical bottlenecks:

  • No manual iframe height clipping: The official Wix App widget automatically resizes inside the Wix Editor canvas and mobile viewport so multi-field B2B forms never get cut off or trap users in double scrollbars.
  • Direct Jotform Tables & CRM pipeline sync: Every quote inquiry lands immediately in a structured Jotform Table with status tags, timestamps, and 1-click export to Excel, Google Sheets, HubSpot, or Close CRM.
  • Generous entry limits on Starter (Free) and Bronze ($34/mo): You can test the complete Wix workflow on Jotform Starter ($0/month for up to 5 active forms and 100 monthly submissions) before scaling to Bronze (25 forms, 1,000 submissions/mo) or Gold ($99/month annual with 10,000 submissions/mo and HIPAA BAA support).

Common questions

Is the Jotform Wix App free to install on a Wix website?

Yes. The Jotform Wix App is free to install from the Wix App Market and works with Jotform's free Starter plan (which includes up to 5 active forms and 100 monthly submissions). To remove Jotform branding or accept up to 1,000 monthly submissions, Bronze costs $34/month billed annually ($39/month billed monthly).

How do I connect my existing Jotform form to a Wix page?

Install the Jotform app from the Wix App Market, click Settings on the Jotform widget inside the Wix Editor, sign in to your Jotform account when prompted, and select your form from the list. Any changes you make later in Jotform Form Builder sync automatically to your live Wix page.

Which Jotform plan do I need if my Wix site collects HIPAA-regulated health information?

You need Jotform Gold ($99/month billed annually or $129/month billed monthly), which includes HIPAA compliance features and a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) alongside 10,000 monthly submissions.
