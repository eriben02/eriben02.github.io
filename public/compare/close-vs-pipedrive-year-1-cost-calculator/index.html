  • Close Forms (all plans) is a form builder. Pipedrive LeadBooster adds chatbot, live chat and Prospector, so lead capture is not the same product on both sides.
  • The Close power dialer is built in on Growth and Scale. Pipedrive needs a Marketplace app; this page prices Aircall Professional, the Aircall plan with a power dialer.
  • Close Solo does not include email open tracking, so email tracking moves Close to Essentials.
  • Aircall outbound calls are unlimited in US and Canada (terms apply). Close bills call minutes on top of seats.

Common questions

Is Close or Pipedrive cheaper for a team that makes calls?

It depends on plan needs and call volume. For 5 reps on annual billing with email tracking, click-to-call and 500 billed minutes per rep per month (an example assumption), Close Essentials comes to $2,760 and Pipedrive Growth + Aircall Essentials to $4,428 in Year 1. With sequences, a power dialer and 1,000 minutes, Close Growth comes to $7,200 and Pipedrive Growth + Aircall Professional to $5,628.

Does Pipedrive have a built-in dialer?

No. Pipedrive's help center says it doesn't have a built-in calling integration and that calls are made through a calling app from its Marketplace, such as Aircall, CloudTalk, Kixie, JustCall or CallHippo. This calculator prices Aircall as the reference dialer: Essentials at $30 and Professional at $50 per license per month billed annually, with a minimum of 3 licenses.

How much do calls cost in Close?

Close says most outbound calls cost around $0.02 per minute, varying by country and number type. Each call is rounded up to the next full minute, so billed minutes are at least your talk time. Phone numbers start at about $1 per month.

Do Close or Pipedrive have a seat minimum?

Neither states a seat minimum, and Close Solo is limited to 1 user. Pipedrive charges per seat whether the seat is assigned to a user or not. The Aircall dialer requires at least 3 licenses, so a 1-rep Pipedrive team pays for 3.

Annual vs monthly billing: how much do you save?

On Close, annual billing saves 7% to 53% depending on the plan (Scale $139 vs $149 per seat per month; Solo $9 vs $19). Pipedrive's pricing page says annual billing saves up to 42%. Pipedrive's monthly prices are not verified here, so monthly totals for Pipedrive are shown as a minimum and marked unverified.

When is Pipedrive Premium cheaper than Growth + LeadBooster?

Only at 1 seat on annual billing: Premium costs $708 a year and Growth + LeadBooster $858. At 2 seats Growth + LeadBooster is lower ($1,326 vs $1,416), because LeadBooster is priced per company (from $32.50 per month) while Premium adds $20 per seat per month.

Which Close plan includes the power dialer?

Growth and Scale ($99 and $139 per seat per month billed annually). The predictive dialer is Scale only.

Are prices in USD and do they include VAT?

All prices are in USD from the vendors' US pricing pages. Pipedrive states its prices are VAT exclusive. Close's VAT treatment is unknown here. Pipedrive may show localized prices outside the US.
