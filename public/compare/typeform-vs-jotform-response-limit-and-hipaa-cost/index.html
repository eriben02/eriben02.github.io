Common questions

Does Typeform offer a HIPAA BAA on Plus or Business plans in 2026?

No. Typeform only signs a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) on custom Enterprise contracts, whereas Jotform includes a HIPAA BAA on its self-serve Gold plan at $99/month billed annually ($1,188/year).

How many monthly submissions are included in Jotform Gold vs. Typeform Business?

Both Jotform Gold ($99/mo annual) and Typeform Business ($83/mo annual) include 10,000 monthly submissions, but only Jotform Gold includes HIPAA compliance and signed BAA at that price.
