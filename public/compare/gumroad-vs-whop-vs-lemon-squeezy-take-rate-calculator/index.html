What we found in the fine print

  • Gumroad’s “10%” is really 12.9% + 80¢ on card sales. Gumroad’s own help center says the 10% + $0.50 fee “does not include” credit card processing (2.9% + $0.30) or PayPal fees.
  • Gumroad Discover costs 30%. Sales from new customers who find you through Gumroad’s marketplace pay a flat 30%, processing included. Whop charges 0% on its Discover marketplace.
  • Lemon Squeezy’s 5% + 50¢ grows with your buyer mix. International buyers add 1.5%, PayPal adds 1.5% and subscription payments add 0.5%, so a non-US PayPal subscriber costs 8.5% + 50¢.
  • Whop’s 2.7% does not include tax. For direct sales, “Whop collects and remits tax” (US, EU, UK) or “Whop collects tax, I remit” costs 2% per transaction; otherwise tax compliance stays with you.
  • Disputes: Lemon Squeezy and Whop each charge a flat $15 per dispute. Gumroad lists no dispute fee, but holds 25% of your balance if chargebacks exceed 1.5% of sales over 365 days.
  • Lemon Squeezy’s future runs through Stripe. In its January 28, 2026 update, Lemon Squeezy said its goal is to give users an easy way to migrate to Stripe Managed Payments, which adds 3.5% per transaction on top of standard Stripe processing fees.

Which platform should you pick?

  • Whop has the lowest base rate (2.7% + 30¢) and charges 0% on marketplace sales, so it wins for mostly-US buyer mixes even with the 2% tax option. Watch the stack on international sales: 2.7% + 1.5% + 1% FX + 2% tax = 7.2% + 30¢, which is more than Lemon Squeezy’s 6.5% + 50¢ on non-US card sales priced above roughly $15–$29 (the break-even depends on whether Whop’s $0.10 Radar + 3DS fees apply). It fits sellers who either want Whop’s 2% managed tax option or already handle sales tax/VAT themselves. Budget for possible reserves on a new account.
  • Lemon Squeezy is a full merchant of record at 5% + 50¢ with license keys and software subscriptions built in. Weigh the Stripe Managed Payments migration path before building a new business on it.
  • Gumroad costs the most on direct sales (12.9% + 80¢ on card sales) but is the simplest way to launch with tax handled. Its fee drops to 5% + 50¢ (plus processing) on direct sales once you pass $20,000 in a month.

Common questions

How much does Gumroad take per sale in 2026?

As checked on Oct 26, 2026, Gumroad charges 10% + $0.50 on sales through your profile or direct links, plus credit card processing of 2.9% + $0.30, so a card sale costs 12.9% + $0.80. Sales through the Gumroad Discover marketplace cost a flat 30% including processing. Once paid sales reach $20,000 in a calendar month, new direct sales that month pay 5% + $0.50 (plus processing).

What are Whop's fees for sellers?

Whop charges 2.7% + $0.30 per successful domestic card transaction, plus 1.5% for international cards and 1% when currency conversion applies. Optional add-ons are 2% for managed tax, 0.5% for Billing and 0.8% for orchestration. Disputes cost $15 each, and a next-day ACH payout costs $2.50. Marketplace (Discover) sales carry 0% commission.

Does Lemon Squeezy really charge only 5% + 50¢?

The base platform fee is 5% + 50¢, but Lemon Squeezy's docs add 1.5% for transactions outside the US, 1.5% for PayPal and 0.5% for subscription payments. Payouts to non-US bank accounts cost 1%, and each chargeback carries a $15 dispute fee.

Which is cheapest: Gumroad, Whop or Lemon Squeezy?

In our example ($29 product, 200 sales a month, 30% international, 10% PayPal), fees are $908/month on Gumroad, $440 on Lemon Squeezy and $421 on Whop with its 2% tax service ($305 without it). Whop is cheapest per transaction; Gumroad is the most expensive on direct sales.

Are Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy and Whop merchants of record?

Gumroad has been merchant of record for all sales since January 1, 2025, and Lemon Squeezy is merchant of record for every sale; both include sales tax and VAT handling in their fee. Whop handles tax on Discover marketplace sales. For direct sales you choose 'Whop collects and remits tax' (US, EU and UK, 2%), 'Whop collects tax, I remit' (2%) or handling tax yourself.

Is Lemon Squeezy shutting down after the Stripe acquisition?

No shutdown date has been announced. Stripe acquired Lemon Squeezy in 2024, and in a January 28, 2026 update Lemon Squeezy said its goal is to give users an easy way to migrate to Stripe Managed Payments, Stripe's merchant of record product, which adds 3.5% per transaction on top of standard Stripe processing fees.

How long do Gumroad and Whop hold payouts?

Gumroad requires a $100 minimum balance and holds each sale at least 7 days on weekly, monthly and quarterly schedules; new accounts go through a 1–3 week review. Whop makes sale proceeds withdrawable in 1–4 business days but can place rolling or fixed reserves on new or high-risk accounts; its Seller Terms cap reserves at 180 days.
