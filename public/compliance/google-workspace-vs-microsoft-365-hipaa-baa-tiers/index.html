Google Workspace vs. Microsoft 365 HIPAA BAA Guide (2026): Exact Minimum Editions & Form Intake Rules

In 2026 the cheapest edition you can run under a HIPAA BAA costs $7 per user per month on both suites: Google Workspace Business Starter on the Annual plan and Microsoft 365 Business Basic paid yearly (sources: Google Workspace pricing and Microsoft 365 pricing pages, verified Oct 27, 2026). The real cost gap opens when you need retention and legal hold, pay month to month, or add a dedicated HIPAA intake form.

The short version: Google lets a super admin accept its HIPAA Business Associate Amendment in the Admin console on paid Workspace editions (only the legacy free edition is excluded). Microsoft includes its HIPAA BAA by default in the Data Protection Addendum for covered entities and business associates, with nothing to click. For 10 users on annual terms, the entry tier costs $840/yr on either suite. If you need retention and holds, Google requires Business Plus ($22, Vault included) and Microsoft offers Business Premium ($22), both $2,640/yr, or the narrower Business Basic + Exchange Online Archiving ($7 + $3) at $1,200/yr. Native Google Forms and Microsoft Forms are both on the BAA service lists; a dedicated HIPAA form tool such as Jotform Gold adds $1,188/yr (annual billing) for e-signatures, payments and encrypted intake.

What the official terms say in 2026

  • Google accepts the BAA in the Admin console: a super administrator goes to Account → Account settings → Legal and compliance, opens the Google Workspace/Cloud Identity HIPAA Business Associate Amendment, answers three questions and clicks OK. A screenshot of that page is Google's stated proof of acceptance.
  • Microsoft's BAA is automatic: Microsoft states the HIPAA BAA is available through the Microsoft Online Services Data Protection Addendum by default to all customers who are covered entities or business associates. You download the DPA or BAA from the Service Trust Portal for your files.
  • Google covered services: Gmail, Calendar, Drive (including Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms), Meet, Chat, Groups, Keep, Sites, Tasks, AppSheet, Gemini in Workspace, Vault (if applicable) and Voice (managed users only). Third-party apps and Marketplace add-ons are not covered.
  • Microsoft covered services (commercial): Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive for Business, Forms, Planner, Power Apps, Power BI, Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Chat, Entra ID and more.
  • Retention is the tier trap: Google Vault is not included in Business Starter or Standard. Microsoft 365 Business Basic and Standard do not include In-Place Hold, Litigation Hold or manual retention policies; Business Premium does, and Basic or Standard can add Exchange Online Archiving for holds and archive.
  • Microsoft raised prices on July 1, 2026: Business Basic went from $6 to $7 and Business Standard from $12.50 to $14; Business Premium stayed at $22. Existing customers keep the old price until renewal.

What admins run into

The most common friction we found is not price but paperwork: small practices on Microsoft 365 cannot find a BAA to sign because there is nothing to sign.

1. "I cannot find the HIPPA/HITECH BAA to review or accept."

A Microsoft 365 Business Basic customer in January 2026, following older guides that pointed to an acceptance screen in the admin center. A Microsoft moderator replied that those pages are no longer available and that the BAA applies by default through the Data Protection Addendum.

Source: Microsoft Q&A, "How to get a BAA with Microsoft Business Basic" (Jan 2026)
2. "If a government authority tells me 'Show us your BAA signed with Microsoft', what do I do? Where is it?"

A Business Standard customer asking where the actual agreement lives. The answer was to download the DPA and follow its link to the full BAA text.

Source: Microsoft Q&A, "Where to Find the Business Associate Agreement..." (Jan 2024)
3. Google's own warning: no BAA, no PHI

Google's help center states that customers who have not signed a BAA must not use PHI in Google Workspace, and that third-party applications including add-ons are not covered by the BAA.

Source: Google Workspace Help, HIPAA Compliance with Google Workspace and Cloud Identity

Setup checklist before any PHI goes in

  1. Google: sign in as super admin, accept the HIPAA Business Associate Amendment under Account settings → Legal and compliance, and save a screenshot.
  2. Microsoft: download the Data Protection Addendum and BAA from the Service Trust Portal and file it with your subscription records.
  3. Turn off what is not covered: disable Marketplace or AppSource add-ons and any service outside the included functionality list for users who handle PHI.
  4. Decide on retention up front: if you need holds or retention rules, budget for Google Business Plus, Microsoft 365 Business Premium or Exchange Online Archiving from day one.
  5. Calendar the renewal: both annual plans auto-renew; Google sends reminders 30 and 3 days before, and annual seats can only be reduced at renewal.

Methodology & assumptions

  • Apples to apples: each calculator row uses the same seat count and billing term on both suites, and pairs tiers by the HIPAA-relevant capability (entry, mid, retention). Remaining feature differences are written into the row itself.
  • Prices: US list prices in USD per user per month, before tax, reseller discounts or nonprofit pricing, checked on official vendor pages on Oct 27, 2026.
  • Microsoft monthly commitment: modeled at 20% above the annual price based on partner price guides, because Microsoft's public US page does not print the month-to-month price. Rows using it are labelled as an estimate.
  • Exchange Online Archiving: $3 per user per month is the annual-commitment list price; for other terms we apply the same 5% or 20% term surcharge as the base plan (estimate).
  • Google intro offer: 50% off the first 20 users for 12 months, shown on Google's pricing page for new customers; applied only to annual terms when switched on.
  • Not modeled: Google Enterprise editions (no public price), Microsoft 365 E3/E5, Copilot add-ons, Teams Phone and Google Voice.

Common questions

Which Google Workspace editions are covered by Google's HIPAA BAA in 2026?

Google's HIPAA Business Associate Amendment can be accepted by a super administrator on paid Google Workspace editions, starting with Business Starter at $7 per user per month on the Annual plan ($8.40 Flexible). Google's help center says only users of the legacy free edition and non-administrators cannot accept it. Vault, which handles retention and legal hold, starts at Business Plus ($22 annual).

How do I accept the Google Workspace BAA?

Sign in to the Google Admin console as a super administrator, go to Account, Account settings, Legal and compliance, open the Google Workspace/Cloud Identity HIPAA Business Associate Amendment, click Review and Accept, answer the three questions and click OK. Google says a screenshot of that page is your proof of acceptance.

Does Microsoft 365 Business Basic include a HIPAA BAA?

Yes. Microsoft states that its HIPAA BAA is available through the Microsoft Online Services Data Protection Addendum by default to all customers who are covered entities or business associates, and Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and Forms are in scope. There is nothing to sign; download the DPA from the Service Trust Portal for your records. Business Basic costs $7 per user per month paid yearly since July 1, 2026.

Is Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 cheaper for HIPAA in 2026?

At list price they tie at every tier: $7 entry (Business Starter vs. Business Basic), $14 mid (Business Standard on both) and $22 for retention (Business Plus vs. Business Premium) per user per month on annual terms. Differences come from billing: Microsoft charges 5% more for annual terms paid monthly, existing Microsoft customers keep $6 and $12.50 until renewal, and Google offers new customers 50% off the first 20 users for 12 months.

Can I use Google Forms or Microsoft Forms for HIPAA patient intake?

Both are listed as covered services: Google Forms under Drive in Google's HIPAA Included Functionality, and Microsoft Forms in Microsoft's in-scope services. A dedicated tool such as Jotform Gold ($99 per month billed annually, $129 monthly) adds HIPAA form features, e-signatures (1,000 signed documents per month) and payment collection on the same form.

Do Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 annual plans auto-renew?

Yes. Google's Annual/Fixed-Term Plan automatically renews for one year if you do nothing, with reminders 30 and 3 days before, and annual licenses can only be reduced at renewal. Microsoft 365 annual subscriptions also auto-renew; there is a 7-day window for a prorated refund, and annual terms paid upfront are not refunded if cancelled early.
