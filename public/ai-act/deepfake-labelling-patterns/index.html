Deepfake labelling patterns

The answer first: Put an “AI” mark where people see it before or as they first see the content: top-right of an image or video, at the start of a video and again after breaks, a short spoken disclaimer at the start of audio, and above or near the headline of text. The EU publishes free icons for this. Using them is optional; disclosing is not. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content · Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Not legal advice. The patterns below summarise Section 2 of the EU Code of Practice and the EU icons page.

The official EU icons (what actually exists)

The European Commission publishes three icons, each in four variants (black, white, black 50 % transparent, white 50 % transparent), as SVG and PNG downloads. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Each icon also comes in white, and in black and white at 50% transparency, as SVG and PNG. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content · EU icons SVG download · EU icons PNG download Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Design rules behind the icons
  • The main element is the capitalised acronym “AI” in English (a national-language acronym is allowed where national language law requires it). Letters have the same height; keep proportions when resizing. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • You may add “generated” or “modified” next to the icon or in a second layer, and say what was changed (for example “face altered”). Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Size, contrast, colour and typography can vary as long as the label stays clear and recognisable. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • The EU says user testing showed better results across all measures when the icon was paired with a text label (for example “modified”). EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
Licence and usage terms

The icons are “publicly available for everyone to use freely, without the need for attribution to the Commission or the AI Office.” Code signatories must follow the placement specifications. Non-signatories using the icons must not present that as adherence to the Code. Using the icon does not by itself establish legal compliance. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Patterns by format

1. Image

Pattern: icon in the top-right corner, embedded in the image, clear of other overlays, readable on any background. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

  • Embed it in the file so it survives reshares and downloads. A UI overlay is acceptable only if it appears to be on the content. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content · Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Prefer the labelled variant (“AI GENERATED” / “AI MODIFIED”) over the bare “AI”. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

2. Video

Pattern: icon at the start, where possible at regular intervals, and at minimum after every interruption (for example ad breaks). Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

  • If only a segment is a deepfake, the Code encourages showing the icon during that segment. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Audio cues alone are not enough for visual deepfakes; they can only be added on top of a visual label. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Live streams: people join late, so repeat the icon. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

3. Audio-only

Pattern: a short spoken disclaimer at the beginning, in plain language (same language as the content, or English). Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

  • Long-form or live audio: add reminders at regular intervals and after interruptions. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • If a screen is available (car display, phone), also show the visual icon. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • An earcon (sound logo) is allowed only with awareness measures, pending a common EU audio solution. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

4. Published text (public-interest topics)

Pattern: icon above or at the top of the text, near the headline, or in the colophon at the start. You may label only the AI-generated part. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

  • Very short text (single words, brief phrases): a contextual notice in the UI is acceptable, for example an indicator next to the output. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • No label needed if a human reviewed it and someone holds editorial responsibility. AI Act Art. 50, Service Desk Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

5. Artistic, satirical, fictional works

Pattern: a lighter disclosure that does not spoil the work: in credits, description, accompanying notes, or adjacent to the frame in the UI; for exhibitions, at the point of entry or on the leaflet or ticket. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

6. Internal-only content (training videos and similar)

Pattern: disclosure may sit in the UI or physical setting before exposure, if it is clear to the people involved. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Accessibility checklist for the label

  • Icon is large enough to see. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Plain-language text, no jargon or abbreviations except “AI”. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • alt text or aria-label saying the content is AI-generated or manipulated. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Timed labels stay up long enough for people with cognitive or processing difficulties. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Second layer (hover or click info) is signalled by the icon and keyboard and screen-reader navigable. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • High contrast; works for colour-vision deficiencies. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Position the icon top-right with clear spacing from other overlays. A CSS overlay is lost when the image is downloaded, so also bake the icon into the exported file. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Common mistakes

  • Label only in the footer or “About” page: not at first exposure. AI Act Art. 50, Service Desk Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Label only on hover: the Code says recognition should not require user action. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • Label only in metadata: machine-readable marks are the provider’s Art. 50(2) duty; deployers still need a visible disclosure. AI Act Art. 50, Service Desk Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
