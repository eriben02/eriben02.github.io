AI Act Article 50: the 2-page labelling checklist for teams that publish AI content.

The two dates people mix up
  • 2 Aug 2026. Article 50 applies and is enforceable. AI Act Service Desk FAQ, when enforcement starts Accessed 30 Oct 2026.
  • 2 Dec 2026. Only for providers of generative AI systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026, and only for machine-readable marking (Art. 50(2)). Visible labels are not delayed. AI Act Service Desk FAQ, when enforcement starts · AI Act Art. 111, Service Desk Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

What the 2-page checklist covers

Do you need to disclose? If you publish deepfakes, run emotion recognition or biometric categorisation on people, or publish unreviewed AI text on public-interest topics, you must tell people, clearly, at first exposure, and accessibly.

Are you a deployer? Using AI in a professional capacity (business, trade, freelance, or regular economic benefit) means you continue. Purely personal, non-professional use only: deployer duties do not apply. Provider duties still do. Commission Guidelines on Art. 50, C(2026) 5054 final Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

  • Emotion recognition or biometric categorisation on people: inform them the system is operating (Art. 50(3)).
  • Image, audio or video deepfake: disclose AI-generated or manipulated (Art. 50(4)).
  • Published AI text informing the public on public-interest matters: disclose, unless human review plus editorial responsibility (Art. 50(4)).

Exceptions. Evidently artistic, satirical or fictional work: lighter, non-intrusive disclosure, still required. Authorised by law for criminal-offence detection or prosecution: no disclosure. Text only, human-reviewed, with a named person who holds editorial responsibility: no disclosure.

How to label it (EU Code of Practice, Section 2). Image: icon top-right, embedded, clear of overlays. Video: icon at the start, at regular intervals, and after every break. Audio: short spoken disclaimer at the start; reminders in long or live audio; visual icon too if a screen exists. Text: icon above the text, near the headline, or in the colophon. Art and satire: in credits, description, or next to the frame. Visible at first exposure, no hover or click needed. Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Icon. Optional, free, no attribution. Basic “AI”, “AI GENERATED”, and “AI MODIFIED”, in black or white, solid or 50 % transparent. Add a text label (“generated”, “modified”). EU testing found this improved results. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Accessibility. alt text or aria-label, plain language, high contrast, timed labels long enough, and a second layer that keyboard and screen-reader users can reach. EU icons for labelling AI-generated content Accessed 30 Oct 2026.

Evidence to keep. Who is responsible for labelling, and where the rule is written down. Real examples of labels in use. For the text exception: name, role and contact of the editorially responsible person, and how review works.
