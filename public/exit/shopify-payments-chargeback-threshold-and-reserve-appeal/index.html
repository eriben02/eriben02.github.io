What Shopify, Visa, and Mastercard actually print

1. The card rate is the sticker US online standard cards are 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic, 2.7% + 30¢ on Grow, 2.5% + 30¢ on Advanced, and 2.25% + 30¢ on Plus. Premium cards and international cards cost more. That extra is not in this sticker.
2. The $15 fee comes back only if you win Shopify's US chargeback fee is $15, and the help center says the fee is returned if you win. This model uses a 20% manual win rate as a planning benchmark, not a Shopify promise. Staff time is not priced here.
3. The 90 days are a lookback, not a hold length An NDRP-inclusive chargeback rate of 1.5% or higher over the last 90 days can auto-enroll the store. Enrollment applies a 10% reserve and resolves disputes automatically: the order is reimbursed, a fee is charged, and you cannot submit evidence. If the 90-day rate is still above 1% after that reserve expires, Shopify says further reserves or payout holds may follow. The reserve's length is in the email, not in a published 90-day rule.

Network lines, cited from Shopify's monitoring guide and Visa's VAMP fact sheet: Visa's excessive merchant ratio is 1.5% with at least 1,500 combined fraud reports and disputes outside CEMEA (2.2%, 150 events, and $75,000 in CEMEA). Shopify's table also lists a non-compliant column at 0.5% and a count of 5. Mastercard ECM is 100 to 299 chargebacks and a 1.5% to 2.99% ratio. HECM is 300 or more and 3% or higher. One test without the other does not place you in ECM. A count of disputes on all cards is not the same as Visa-only or Mastercard-only volume, so this page flags the ratio and tells you the count test is separate.

Sources: Shopify pricing, Shopify chargeback fees, Shopify NDRP, Shopify reserves, Shopify network monitoring, Visa VAMP fact sheet.

Common questions

Does Shopify publish a 0.75% dispute threshold or a 90-day payout hold?

No. Shopify's help center does not print a 0.75% dispute line. The 0.75% rate on the US Shopify Plus plan is the multi-currency payout fee, which is a different charge. The 90 days in the NDRP article are the window used to measure the chargeback rate. Reserve length is sent by email. Shopify does not publish a rule that every reserve lasts 90 days.

When does Shopify Payments apply a 10% reserve?

If a store is enrolled in a network dispute resolution program because of a high chargeback rate, Shopify says a 10% reserve is applied to Shopify Payments payouts. Merchants with an NDRP-inclusive chargeback rate of 1.5% or higher over the last 90 days can be enrolled automatically. If the 90-day rate is still above 1% after that reserve expires, Shopify says further reserves or payout holds may follow. Other reserves can still be applied for risk reasons that are not a published percent.

What are the Visa VAMP and Mastercard ECM lines?

Shopify's monitoring guide lists Visa VAMP excessive thresholds of 1.5% and 1,500 combined fraud reports and disputes outside Central Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, and 2.2% with 150 events and $75,000 there. The same table lists a non-compliant column at 0.5% and a count of 5. Visa's own fact sheet says the excessive merchant ratio in AP, Canada, EU, and the US drops to 150 basis points on 1 April 2026, and that this merchant test applies when the acquirer is not already Above Standard or Excessive. Mastercard ECM needs both 100 to 299 chargebacks and a 1.5% to 2.99% ratio. HECM needs 300 or more and 3% or higher. This calculator can see your all-card dispute rate. It cannot see Visa-only or Mastercard-only counts.
