What we are using for the fee facts

  • Stripe: a cardholder dispute is modeled at a $15 fee. Stripe has also described a separate $15 counter fee when a dispute is challenged without Chargeback Protection. That counter fee is named in the calculation notes and is not added on top of the $15 x disputes line.
  • Shopify Payments: modeled at $15 per dispute. Shopify refunds that fee in the US when you win. In several other regions the fee is not returned. This calculator keeps the fee in the loss so the total is not optimistic.
  • Paddle: public notes put the dispute fee around $15 to $20. The Paddle option uses $20.
  • High-volume or high-risk: modeled at $25 per dispute, before any monitoring program penalty.
  • Chargeflow: $0 monthly and $0 setup in this model, then 25% of recovered dispute dollars only. The 50% recovery rate is a planning benchmark so the fee is easy to audit. Ask Chargeflow for the win rate on your category before you treat the savings as a forecast.
  • Percent of all sales: 0.40% of yearly GMV. That is the comparison for a protection product billed on every dollar, not only on disputes. Stripe Radar for Fraud Teams is a different product and is often billed per screened payment. We do not treat 0.40% as Radar's only price.
  • Shopify Protect: covers eligible Shop Pay orders in the US. It does not cover a standard card gateway or PayPal dispute.

Sources: Stripe disputes, Stripe Radar, Shopify chargebacks, Chargeflow.

Common questions

How much is a Stripe or Shopify dispute fee?

This calculator uses $15 per dispute for Stripe and for Shopify Payments. Shopify refunds that fee in the US when you win. In several other regions it does not. Paddle is modeled at $20, inside a $15 to $20 range. A high-risk processor is modeled at $25.

What does Chargeflow charge?

In this model Chargeflow has a $0 monthly fee and a $0 setup fee, then 25% of the dispute dollars it recovers. The 50% recovery figure is a planning benchmark so you can audit the fee. It is not a guaranteed win rate.

Why compare a 0.40% fee on all sales?

0.40% of yearly sales is $11,520 on $2.88 million. A 25% fee on only the dollars actually recovered is much smaller when disputes are a fraction of orders. Stripe Radar for Fraud Teams is often priced per screened payment, so do not read 0.40% as its only rate.
