What changes the list price

  • PandaDoc Free allows 5 documents a month on one shared limit. It is not a row above.
  • Documents sent from the PandaDoc editor are not metered on Starter or Business. Documents created through the API, Zapier, Make, forms, or bulk send use usage credits. The recurring credit pack price is not published. The API stops when credits run out. Other automations continue, and the overage uses the unit price on the order form, or up to $5 a document when the order form has no unit price. Unused documents do not roll over.
  • A 14-day PandaDoc trial is listed and is not subtracted here. PandaDoc prices exclude taxes. Qualified electronic signatures are on annual PandaDoc plans only.
  • HIPAA is not listed on PandaDoc Starter or Business. A third-party summary calls it an optional Enterprise extra, so this page treats it as a quote. Third-party PandaDoc pricing summary Listed 14 Oct 2026. Accessed 13 Nov 2026.
  • PandaDoc annual plans renew unless cancellation is requested at least 30 days before renewal. The company says a reminder goes out 45 days ahead.
  • DocuSign annual plans include 100 envelopes per user for the year. Month-to-month plans include 10 per user each month. A sent envelope counts even when it is not completed.
  • DocuSign also sells annual plans billed monthly: Personal $11 a month ($132 a year), Standard $30 per user ($360), and Business Pro $45 per user ($540). This calculator uses annual upfront or month-to-month, not that middle option.
  • An annual DocuSign plan is a 12-month commitment. Cancel within 30 days of purchase for a refund, once per customer. After 30 days, an annual plan billed monthly still owes the rest of the year. Plans renew on their own.
  • DocuSign Personal is one user and 5 envelopes a month. Past that, extra envelopes are not offered on Personal. Standard and Business Pro list up to 50 users. Web forms, bulk send, and payment collection are on Business Pro.
  • SMS delivery starts at $0.36 a delivery and ID verification starts at $2.40 an attempt. Those add-ons are not in this total. A HIPAA agreement, SSO, and knowledge-based authentication are on Enhanced plans, which are a quote.
  • Jotform Sign monthly limits are 10 on Starter, 100 on Bronze, 250 on Silver, and 1,000 on Gold. Paid self-serve plans are one user. Enterprise is the multi-user plan, and its price is a quote. One shared account is not the same thing as one account per person.
  • Jotform HIPAA features are on Gold and Enterprise. Plans renew until canceled. A 30-day money-back guarantee is listed, and canceling keeps the plan until the next billing date. A nonprofit or education discount, and any 24-hour signup offer, are not in these prices.
