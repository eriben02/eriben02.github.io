Does HubSpot Charge Onboarding Fees in 2026? (Exact Fee Schedule & Waivers)

Yes. HubSpot charges a mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee for Sales Hub Professional ($3,500 for Enterprise) and a mandatory $3,000 onboarding fee for Marketing Hub Professional ($7,000 for Enterprise) in 2026 (source: HubSpot official pricing & product catalog, verified Oct 2026).

Common questions

Can you waive HubSpot's $1,500 or $3,000 onboarding fee in 2026?

HubSpot waives its direct onboarding fee only if you sign up through an accredited HubSpot Solutions Partner agency (who bills their own retainer) or negotiate a multi-hub annual contract at quarter-end.

Do HubSpot Starter plans charge an onboarding fee?

No. HubSpot Starter ($15/seat/month annual) has $0 mandatory onboarding fee; mandatory onboarding starts on Professional and Enterprise tiers.
