How many envelopes are included in DocuSign Standard and Business Pro in 2026?

Both DocuSign Standard ($25/user/mo annual) and Business Pro ($40/user/mo annual) include an annual allowance of 100 sent envelopes per user per year (about 8 envelopes per month).

Which alternatives avoid per-envelope overage fees?

Jotform Sign (included in Jotform Gold at $99/mo flat with HIPAA compliance) and PandaDoc Business ($49/seat/mo annual) allow high-volume e-signature workflows without a 100-envelope annual cap.
