What is not in the Year-1 figure

  • The manual response total covers only Stripe's fees. It does not include the time your team spends preparing evidence.
  • Smart Disputes is in public preview. Eligibility depends on the reason code, the payment method, and whether evidence is available. This total assumes the dispute is eligible. A dispute that is not eligible is unknown here.
  • The appeal fee is $15 and is not returned even if you win. This page does not ask how many appeals you file, so appeal fees stay unknown.
  • Chargeflow Insights is $0. Prevent is $0.10 per scanned transaction, and the first 1,000 scanned transactions are free. Alerts are $29 per deflected chargeback. A lower Alerts price above 50 a month is not published, so that discount is unknown. None of those products are in the Automation total above.
  • Chargeflow says eligible ecommerce and SaaS startups can get up to 70% off. Who qualifies is not stated, so the discount is unknown and is not applied.
  • Chargeflow's "4x ROI Guarantee" is marketing. It is not a measured result and it is not used here.

Stripe Chargeback Protection

Stripe Chargeback Protection is not listed on the US pricing page checked 11 Nov 2026. The old product page redirects to Radar, which does not mention it. Terms last updated 3 June 2019 are still online and say the fee is sent by email or shown in the Dashboard. Current availability and the current price are unknown. Check your own Dashboard. A 2019 launch price is not used here. One Stripe user wrote on 18 Oct 2025 that Stripe said the service was being discontinued and refunded the charges. That is one account, not a Stripe notice.
