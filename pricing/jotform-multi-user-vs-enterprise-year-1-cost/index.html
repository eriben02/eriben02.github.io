What applies to a team

  • A standard plan has a 30-day money-back guarantee: a full refund if you cancel within 30 days of payment. You can cancel at any time. It is a recurring subscription.
  • Enterprise has no month-to-month price. Fees for the whole term are paid up front. There is no trial and no refund. Users added during the term are prorated.
  • Jotform staff say a standard plan cannot add seats, and sharing a login is against the Terms of Use. Enterprise requires a unique person and email for each user, does not allow group aliases, includes up to 5 users in the base package, and sells extra users in bundles of 5. Jotform staff answer on multi-user accounts Accessed 15 Nov 2026. Jotform staff answer on adding a user Accessed 15 Nov 2026.
  • HIPAA features are unavailable on Starter, Bronze, and Silver, and available on Gold and Enterprise. Eligible customers with HIPAA features enabled can sign a BAA with Jotform.
  • On a HIPAA server, administrators only see submission data that was explicitly shared with them. The HIPAA server add-on price, and any extra BAA or HIPAA fee on Enterprise, are unknown.
  • Whether several separate individual accounts are fine in practice when an organization wants shared data is unknown.
  • On Reddit, a small team asked why they cannot have separate individual accounts. This page uses only that question. Reddit thread Accessed 15 Nov 2026.
