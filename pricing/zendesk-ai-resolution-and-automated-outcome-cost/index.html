What Zendesk’s $1.50–$2.00 AI resolution fee adds to your monthly invoice.

Zendesk Suite includes just 5 to 10 automated resolutions per agent each month—and pay-as-you-go overage at $2.00 per verified resolution is turned on by default. Run your ticket volume below.

The short version: Zendesk includes 5 automated resolutions per agent/month on Suite Team ($55/agent annual, $69 monthly) and 10 on Suite Professional ($115 annual, $149 monthly), capped at 10,000 per year. Beyond that allowance, each automated resolution costs $1.50 if pre-committed or $2.00 pay-as-you-go. For 10 agents on Suite Professional handling 5,000 conversations/month with a 40% verified AI resolution rate (2,000 resolutions), you pay $1,150 in seats + $3,800 in AI overage = $4,950/mo ($59,400/yr) at $2.00, or $4,000/mo at $1.50—compared with roughly $3,000/mo on Freshdesk Pro ($49 per 100 AI sessions) and $1,950/mo on Help Scout Plus ($0.75/resolution).

What we found in Zendesk’s pricing & help-center terms

  • The included allowance is tiny: 5 automated resolutions/agent/month on Suite Team or Support Team, and 10 on Suite Professional ("up to 10k per year").
  • Overage rate: $1.50 per committed automated resolution; $2.00 per pay-as-you-go resolution.
  • Overage is enabled by default: Zendesk’s help center lists "Allow overage (default setting)", billed monthly regardless of subscription term. Switching to "Don't allow overage" pauses AI agents once the allowance runs out.
  • Only LLM-verified resolutions are billed: Since May 18, 2026 Zendesk splits outcomes into Assisted escalation, Contained resolution, and Verified resolution; only Verified resolutions count against your allowance.
  • Two allowance models exist: The public pricing page lists resolution counts, while accounts on the newer "resolution allowance" model receive a dollar pool ($2/agent/month on Team, $5 on Professional, $10 on Enterprise; max $5,000/year; unused allowance does not roll over).
  • 30-day non-renewal notice: Subscriptions auto-renew for an equivalent term at then-current rates unless you send 30 days’ written notice to revops@zendesk.com (Customer Agreement §7.2, §7.4).

Checklist to keep a Zendesk AI bill predictable

  1. Set your overage rule on day one: Go to Admin Center → Account → Usage → Overage. "Allow overage" is the default; "Don't allow overage" pauses AI agents at the limit.
  2. Pre-commit if you know you will exceed the allowance: Committed resolutions cost $1.50 vs. $2.00 pay-as-you-go—a 25% reduction on every resolution above your allowance.
  3. Watch the 80% warning alert: Zendesk sends an in-product warning and email at 80% usage. Deleting a ticket does not refund a consumed automated resolution.
  4. Set a calendar reminder 45 days before renewal: Non-renewal requires 30 days’ written notice to revops@zendesk.com.

Common questions

How much does Zendesk charge per AI automated resolution in 2026?

As verified on Oct 25, 2026, Zendesk charges $1.50 per committed (pre-purchased) automated resolution and $2.00 per pay-as-you-go resolution once the included allowance is used. Suite Team includes 5 and Suite Professional 10 automated resolutions per agent per month (up to 10,000 per year).

Is Zendesk AI overage turned on by default?

Yes. Zendesk's help center lists 'Allow overage' as the default setting, and overage is billed monthly regardless of your subscription term. Admins can choose 'Don't allow overage' under Admin Center > Account > Usage > Overage, which pauses AI agents when the allowance is exhausted.

What counts as a billable Zendesk automated resolution?

Since May 18, 2026 Zendesk classifies AI outcomes as Assisted escalation, Contained resolution or Verified resolution. Only Verified resolutions, which pass an LLM check that the request was satisfactorily resolved, consume your allowance. Email conversations end 72 hours after the first email; messaging conversations end after 2 hours by default.

How much does Zendesk Suite cost per agent in 2026?

Suite Team costs $55 per agent per month billed annually ($69 monthly) and Suite Professional costs $115 annually ($149 monthly). Support Team costs $19 annually ($25 monthly). Suite Enterprise is sales-quoted. The Copilot add-on is $50 per agent per month billed annually.

Is Freshdesk or Help Scout cheaper than Zendesk for AI support?

At list prices for 10 agents, 5,000 conversations a month and a 40% AI resolution rate, Zendesk Suite Professional costs about $4,950/month at $2.00 overage, Freshdesk Pro about $3,000/month ($55 seats + $49 per 100 AI sessions) and Help Scout Plus about $1,950/month ($45 seats + $0.75 per AI resolution).

How do I cancel or avoid auto-renewal of a Zendesk subscription?

Under section 7.2 of the Zendesk Customer Agreement, subscriptions renew automatically for an equivalent term, possibly at then-current rates, unless either party gives at least 30 days' prior written notice. Customers notify Zendesk by emailing revops@zendesk.com.
