What do HubSpot Payments fees cost versus connecting Stripe?

This page compares HubSpot payments with Stripe connected to HubSpot.

Your payments

HubSpot payments is listed for businesses in the US, UK, and Canada. Another country uses a connected Stripe account.

Used only for a UK business. The FAQ lists both adders.

Default is Stripe's standard US domestic card rate, 2.9% + $0.30, checked 12 Nov 2026. Change it if your rate is different.

Assumption. The 0% platform fee in Year 1 is a promotion for eligible customers. Who qualifies is not stated, so this stays off unless you turn it on. Year 2 uses 0.5% either way.

The 0.75% platform fee applies on top of Stripe's own fees. On bank debits, that 0.75% is uncapped.

What is not in these fees

  • Currency conversion on HubSpot payments is +1% in the US and +2% in the UK and Canada. It is not in this total.
  • Affirm and Klarna are 5.99% plus the platform fee. They are not in this total.
  • HubSpot charges no refund fee. The original transaction fees are not returned.
  • Taxes may apply to payment processing fees from 3 Aug 2026. Whether they apply here is unknown.
  • A high-volume rate is possible through HubSpot's commerce team. The terms are unknown.
  • Stripe's dispute received fee is $15. This page does not ask how many disputes you get, so dispute fees stay out of the total.
  • Stripe lists +1.5% for international cards when currency conversion is required. It is not added unless you include it in the Stripe rate you enter.
  • Outside the US, Stripe's own bank-debit fee was not on the US pricing page checked 12 Nov 2026, so it stays unknown and is not added.
  • The Revenue Hub seat price is unknown and is not in this total. Invoices, payment links, and subscriptions have no monthly SaaS fee. Quotes and contracts need a Revenue Hub subscription.
  • Payouts can be held during a document request or a risk review. A maximum monthly volume is set in underwriting.
  • Downgrading to the free HubSpot plan ends HubSpot payments. Subscriptions keep billing for 30 days.

What users wrote

  • On the HubSpot Community, a prospective customer calls the Stripe option's platform fee a dealbreaker. That is one post, not a measured result. HubSpot Community thread Accessed 12 Nov 2026.
