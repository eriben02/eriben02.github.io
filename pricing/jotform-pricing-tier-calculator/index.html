Jotform Pricing Tier Calculator (2026): Starter, Bronze, Silver & Gold True Year-1 Cost

In 2026, Jotform costs $0/month on Starter (100 submissions/mo), $34/month billed annually on Bronze (1,000 submissions/mo), $39/month billed annually on Silver (2,500 submissions/mo), and $99/month billed annually on Gold with a signed HIPAA BAA and 10,000 submissions/month (source: Jotform official pricing page, verified Oct 2026). Because Silver costs only $5/month ($60/year) more than Bronze on annual billing while increasing monthly submissions 2.5x and storage 10x, Silver is the sweet spot for growing B2B teams.

Common questions

How much does Jotform cost per month in 2026?

On annual billing in 2026, Jotform Starter is free ($0/mo for 5 forms and 100 submissions/mo), Bronze is $34/mo (25 forms, 1,000 submissions/mo), Silver is $39/mo (50 forms, 2,500 submissions/mo), and Gold is $99/mo (100 forms, 10,000 submissions/mo, plus HIPAA BAA eligibility).

Does Jotform charge extra to embed forms on Wix?

No. The official Jotform Wix App is free to install from the Wix App Market across all Jotform tiers, including the free Starter plan.
