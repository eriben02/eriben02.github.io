Stripe Billing 0.7% Recurring Fee Calculator (2026): True Cost vs. Whop & Paddle

In 2026, Stripe charges an extra 0.7% on recurring subscription volume (Stripe Billing) plus 0.5% for automated tax calculation (Stripe Tax) on top of 2.9% + $0.30 card processing, pushing effective recurring take rates to ~4.1% + $0.30 per charge versus 2.7% + $0.30 base card processing on Whop (sources: Stripe, Whop & Paddle pricing pages, verified Oct 2026).

Add dispute fees to this Year-1 picture

Processing rates are only part of the bill. Each chargeback also takes the order value plus a dispute fee, usually $15 on Stripe and Shopify Payments, and about $30 of evidence prep if you fight it in house. Common questions

How much does Stripe Billing charge for recurring subscriptions in 2026?

Stripe Billing pay-as-you-go charges 0.7% of recurring subscription volume on top of standard 2.9% + $0.30 card processing fees. Adding Stripe Tax adds another 0.5% per transaction.

Does Whop charge an extra 0.7% fee on recurring subscriptions?

No. Whop's domestic card processing starts at 2.7% + $0.30 without a separate 0.7% recurring billing surcharge.
