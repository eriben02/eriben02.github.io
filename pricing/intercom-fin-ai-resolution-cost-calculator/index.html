What Intercom’s $0.99 AI resolution fee actually does to your support bill.

Seat licenses start at $29 to $132 a month, but Fin AI adds $0.99 every time it resolves a ticket. Here is how fast the variable meter outgrows your seat bill—and how session-pack helpdesks compare.

The short version: Intercom charges $0.99 per Fin AI resolution (including automated resolutions, procedure handoffs, and disqualifications, plus $9.99 per sales qualification) on top of agent seat licenses ($29/mo Essential, $85/mo Advanced, $132/mo Expert billed annually; $39/$99/$139 monthly). For a 5-agent team on Advanced ($425/mo) handling 3,000 tickets/month with a 50% AI resolution rate (1,500 resolutions), Fin AI adds $1,485/mo—bringing the total bill to $1,910/mo ($22,920/yr). By comparison, Freshdesk Pro ($55/agent/mo annual) includes 500 one-time Freddy AI Agent sessions and then bills $49 per 100-session pack (charged per AI session handled, whether resolved or not; Freddy AI Copilot is an optional $29/agent/mo add-on).

Why support teams call $0.99/resolution an "AI tax"

When outcome-based AI meters have no hard cap by default, seasonal support spikes or better help-center articles immediately increase your monthly software invoice.

1. Billable resolution definition includes silent exits after Fin replies

Under Intercom’s Fin AI pricing rules, a billable $0.99 resolution is counted whenever a customer confirms Fin’s answer helped OR leaves the conversation without requesting a human agent after Fin’s response, at most once per conversation.

Common questions

How much does Intercom Fin AI charge per resolution in 2026?

Intercom charges $0.99 per Fin AI outcome/resolution (and $9.99 per sales qualification outcome), billed at most once per conversation, on top of your monthly per-seat plan ($29 Essential, $85 Advanced, or $132 Expert billed annually).

What counts as a billable Fin AI resolution in Intercom?

A billable $0.99 Fin outcome occurs when the customer confirms their question was answered, exits the conversation without requesting a human agent after Fin's answer, or triggers a configured procedure handoff/disqualification outcome.

How does Freshdesk Freddy AI pricing compare to Intercom's $0.99 per-resolution meter?

Freshdesk (by Freshworks) charges $19 (Growth), $55 (Pro), or $89 (Enterprise) per agent/month billed annually ($23/$66/$107 monthly). For autonomous AI support, Freddy AI Agent includes 500 one-time complimentary sessions per account and then bills $49 per 100-session pack (charged per session, resolved or not), while Freddy AI Copilot for human agents is an optional $29/agent/month add-on.
