Common questions

Why does PayPal hold funds for 180 days on a permanent limitation?

Roughly 180 days matches the maximum card-network chargeback window (Visa and Mastercard allow cardholders up to 120–180 days to file a dispute). PayPal holds the balance as collateral until the chargeback liability window expires.

What is PayPal's High Volume Dispute Fee in 2026?

PayPal charges a $15 Standard Dispute Fee, which doubles to a $30 High Volume Dispute Fee if your dispute rate reaches 1.5% or higher with more than 100 transactions in the prior three full months.
